How PackRoute works

Three simple steps from online sale to doorstep delivery.

  1. 1

    Store sends an order

    Your e-commerce platform pushes order details — customer, items, seller location — to our API.

  2. 2

    We prepare & route

    The seller packs the order. We mark it ready and choose the best carrier for that route.

  3. 3

    Customer receives the package

    Israel Post, HFD, FedEx and more — tracked from warehouse to front door.
